Support

For a bug, it helps to include:

Frequently asked questions

What is SALVAGE?
A to-do list where every task has a lifespan. You give each task 1, 3, 7, 15 or 31 days. When its time runs out, it’s gone. Things with a deadline belong in your calendar.
What does SALVAGE mean in the app?
It’s what you can do on a task’s last day, and only then: keep the task, and it moves on to its next lifespan. Each lifespan is shorter than the one before (31 → 15 → 7 → 3 → 1 days), and a task on its 1-day lifespan can’t be salvaged again. Whatever it starts with, no task lives longer than 57 days.
I finished a task. What do I do with it?
Open it and press and hold Let It Go. SALVAGE doesn’t tell finished tasks apart from dropped ones.
When does a lifespan run out?
When its last day ends, at midnight on your device’s clock. There’s no grace period.
Can I bring back a task that has ended?
No. Tasks you let go of and tasks that expired are shown in History for 7 days, but they can’t be brought back. That’s why SALVAGE and Let It Go are decided by pressing and holding.
I’m not getting notifications.
SALVAGE sends one notification, on the morning a task reaches its last day (9:00 by default). On days with no last days, there is none. On your iPhone, check that notifications for SALVAGE are allowed in Settings › Notifications › SALVAGE, and that a Focus isn’t silencing them. You can change the time in the app under Settings › Time.
I don’t want task names on my Lock Screen.
On your iPhone, go to Settings › Notifications › SALVAGE › Show Previews and choose When Unlocked or Never.
Is SALVAGE free?
The free version holds up to 5 tasks at a time. Tasks free up as they end. Lifespans, last days, SALVAGE, notifications and History all work in the free version. SALVAGE Plus, a monthly or yearly subscription, removes the limit and adds the widgets, two more themes (Morning Mist and Nocturne), a choice of last-day scene, and the lifespan new tasks start with. The yearly plan starts with a two-week free trial for new subscribers.
What is a last-day scene?
The photograph on a task’s last day, and when its time runs out. The poppy is free; with SALVAGE Plus you can choose another flower (cosmos, camellia or cherry blossom) in Settings › Last-Day Scene.
How do I change how SALVAGE looks?
In Settings › Appearance. SALVAGE follows your iPhone’s light or dark setting, or you can keep it light or dark. Light and Dark are free; with SALVAGE Plus, the light can be Morning Mist and the dark Nocturne.
What do the widgets show?
With SALVAGE Plus, the Home Screen widget (small or medium) shows how many tasks reach their last day today and their names, or how long until the next last day. The Lock Screen widget shows only the count, never a task name. To add one, touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and choose SALVAGE.
Can new tasks start with a lifespan other than 7 days?
With SALVAGE Plus, choose it in Settings › New Task Lifespan. In the free version new tasks start at 7 days. Either way, you can pick 1, 3, 7, 15 or 31 days for each task as you add it.
How do I restore my purchase?
On an iPhone signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. This also works on a new iPhone.
How do I cancel SALVAGE Plus, and what happens then?
On your iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › SALVAGE, or open Settings › Manage Subscription in the app. After cancelling, SALVAGE Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for. When it ends, nothing is deleted: tasks over the free limit live out their lifespans as usual, and you can add again once you hold fewer than 5. The last-day scene goes back to the poppy, the theme to Light or Dark, new tasks start at 7 days, and the widget shows SALVAGE Plus instead of your tasks. If you subscribe again, your choices come back.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds; the developer can’t issue them. Please request one at Apple’s Report a Problem.
Will my tasks move to a new iPhone, or survive reinstalling the app?
They move with a restore from iCloud Backup or an iPhone-to-iPhone transfer with Quick Start. SALVAGE has no account and no cloud sync, so there is no other way to move them. Deleting the app deletes your tasks.
Is SALVAGE available on iPad or Android?
SALVAGE is made for iPhone. There is no Android version.
What information does SALVAGE send?
Your tasks never leave your device. To make the app better, it sends only anonymous usage statistics, such as opening the app, adding a task or how many tasks you hold, never your tasks’ names. You can turn this off in Settings › Share Usage Statistics. See the Privacy Policy for details.
