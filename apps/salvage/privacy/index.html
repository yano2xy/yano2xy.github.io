SALVAGE

Privacy Policy

How SALVAGE (“the app”) handles information about you.

Your tasks never leave your device.

There is no account, no ads and no tracking. To make SALVAGE better, the app sends anonymous usage statistics that never include your tasks' names. You can turn them off in the app's Settings.

Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer of the SALVAGE app (“the developer”) handles information in the app.

What stays on your device (the developer never receives it)

The following is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party. The developer runs no servers.

  • The tasks you add: their names, lifespans, the day each was added and its last day, how many times it was salvaged, and when and how it ended
  • Your settings, such as the notification time, the last-day scene you chose, whether SALVAGE Plus is active, and whether you have seen the introduction

Ended tasks are shown in History for 7 days and then deleted from your device for good. Data on your device is included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer. Backups are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

Usage statistics (what the developer receives)

To improve the app and understand how its features are used, SALVAGE sends the following to an analytics service, PostHog:

  • What you do in the app (opened it, added a task, salvaged one, let one go, a task’s lifespan ran out, opened or subscribed to SALVAGE Plus, and so on), with the numbers and kinds that go with it (the lifespan in days, how many tasks you hold at a time, how many times a task was salvaged, the scene you chose, and so on)
  • Once a day, the day’s state: how many tasks you hold, how many are on their last day, and whether notifications are allowed and at what time
  • A random identifier made for each installation of the app (unrelated to the advertising identifier, IDFA)
  • Technical information such as the app version, your language and region settings, whether SALVAGE Plus is active, the OS and its version, the device type and the screen size

Your tasks’ names, and anything else you type, are never sent. IP addresses are not stored, and your location is not estimated. Nothing that identifies you, such as your name or email address, is collected.

To stop sending usage statistics, turn off Settings › Share Usage Statistics in the app. It sends one last event saying it was turned off, then nothing more.

Other information the developer receives

  • What you send by email when you contact us, including your email address
  • Diagnostics such as crash reports, provided by Apple only if you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone. They do not identify you

Notifications

SALVAGE’s notifications are local notifications, scheduled and shown on your device. No outside server is involved. A notification includes the task’s name, which may appear on the Lock Screen depending on your iOS settings. To hide it, go to Settings › Notifications › SALVAGE › Show Previews on your iPhone.

Purchases (SALVAGE Plus)

SALVAGE Plus is an auto-renewable subscription, bought, restored and cancelled through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment details. The app talks to the App Store to show the prices and the free trial, and to check whether SALVAGE Plus is active.

How the information is used

The developer uses what it receives only to:

  • Fix bugs and improve the app
  • Understand how features are used, and review what the app offers and what it costs
  • Answer your messages

Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share the information it receives with third parties without your consent, except where required by law. Processing by the service listed under “Third-party services” on the developer’s behalf is the exception.

Third-party services

  • PostHog (usage analytics; data is processed on servers in the United States): https://posthog.com/privacy

SALVAGE contains no advertising, crash-reporting or social media tools (SDKs). It does not collect or share information to track you.

Permissions

The only permission SALVAGE asks for is notifications. Allowing them is optional; the app works without them. It does not use the camera, photos, location or contacts.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can end a task at any time with Let It Go. Ended tasks are deleted for good after 7 days.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device (except copies in your device backups).
  • Usage statistics are kept for PostHog’s retention period. Deleting the app deletes its identifier, so what is sent after reinstalling is not connected to what was sent before.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them. To have yours deleted, write to the address below.

Security

The developer takes necessary and reasonable measures to protect the information it receives from leaks, loss or damage.

Children’s privacy

SALVAGE does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change with the law or with the app. The updated policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
